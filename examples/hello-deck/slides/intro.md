# Welcome to Slidewalk

A tiny slide deck engine with **zero build steps**. Write your slides in
markdown, list them in one HTML file, and open it in your favorite browser.

Then get around with the keyboard:

- **Left / Right**: move through the slides in this lane
- **Up / Down**: hop to another lane
- **P**: toggle presenter mode
- **O**: toggle the zoomed-out overview grid
- **T**: switch between the light and dark theme
- **1-9, 0**: show a meta slide (try **1** now). **Escape** hides it again.
